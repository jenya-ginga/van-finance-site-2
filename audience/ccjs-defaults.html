Van Finance With CCJs or Defaults

A County Court Judgment or default on file makes mainstream lending very unlikely — but it doesn't close every door.

The challenge

A CCJ or default is one of the more serious adverse markers a lender sees, and most mainstream banks will decline automatically. Specialist non-prime lenders will still consider an application, but expect a higher representative APR, and lenders will generally want to know whether the CCJ/default is satisfied and how recent it is.

What tends to help

  • A satisfied CCJ (one you've paid off) is viewed more favourably than an unsatisfied one — settle it if you can before applying.
  • The more recent the CCJ/default, the harder underwriting tends to be; time since the event genuinely helps.
  • Be upfront about it on the application — lenders will see it on the credit check regardless, and consistency helps trust.
  • A larger deposit is one of the more effective levers available in this situation.
