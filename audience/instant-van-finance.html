Instant / Same-Day Van Finance Decisions

Getting a fast decision when you need a van urgently.

The situation

Several UK lenders can give an initial decision within minutes online and pay out to the dealer the same or next working day once documents are verified, particularly for straightforward, well-documented applications. 'Instant' usually refers to the decision, not necessarily the full payout — verification of income, identity and the vehicle can still take a little longer, especially for self-employed applicants.

What tends to help

  • Have proof of income, ID and address documents ready and digitised before you apply, so verification isn't the bottleneck.
  • Self-employed applicants should have SA302s or recent accounts ready — these often take longest to process manually.
  • A clean, well-documented application with no discrepancies between the form and your supporting documents moves fastest.
  • Ask the dealer or lender directly what their current typical turnaround time is, since it varies by lender and time of year.
