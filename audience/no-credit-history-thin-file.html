Credit profile

Van Finance With No Credit History

Not bad credit — just no track record yet. A "thin file" is treated differently to negative history by most lenders.

The challenge

Having no credit history isn't the same as having bad credit, but some lenders' automated scoring can't always tell the difference, which is why thin-file applicants sometimes get declined by mainstream lenders despite having no negative marks at all.

What tends to help

  • Open banking-based affordability checks are particularly well suited to a thin file, since they look at real income and spending rather than a score built on history you don't have yet.
  • Registering on the electoral roll and holding a UK bank account for several months both help build a file quickly.
  • A guarantor can bridge the gap while your own file builds up.
