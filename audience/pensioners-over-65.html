Van Finance for Pensioners & Over-65s

Still working, running a small trade, or helping family with a business van — age alone shouldn't rule out finance, but it does change what lenders look at.

The challenge

Some lenders set a maximum age at the end of the finance agreement (commonly somewhere between 70 and 80), which can rule out a long term for an older applicant even with excellent credit. Income assessment can also differ if you're relying on a pension rather than employment income.

What tends to help

  • Choose a shorter term if a lender's maximum end-of-term age is limiting your options — it often opens up more lenders.
  • Have pension statements or proof of retirement income ready alongside standard ID and address proof.
  • A broker can be particularly useful here, since maximum-age policies vary a lot between individual lenders.
  • If you're still trading self-employed past retirement age, treat the application like any self-employed case — see our self-employed guide.
