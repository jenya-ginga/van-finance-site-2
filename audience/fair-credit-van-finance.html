Fair (Average) Credit Van Finance

Not bad credit, not excellent either — here's where you tend to sit with lenders.

The situation

Fair credit sits in an awkward middle: you're unlikely to be automatically declined the way a poor credit file might be, but you also won't usually get the very best advertised rates reserved for excellent credit. Lenders in this space tend to look more holistically at income, employment stability and existing commitments rather than relying on the credit score alone.

What tends to help

  • Check your credit report before applying — a fair score can sometimes be nudged up quickly by fixing simple issues like an out-of-date address.
  • Compare more than one lender's representative APR — the spread for 'fair' credit tends to be wider than for excellent credit.
  • A larger deposit or shorter term can offset a mid-range score and bring the monthly payment or total cost down.
  • Avoid multiple applications in a short window while shopping around — use eligibility checkers with soft searches first.
