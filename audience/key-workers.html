Van Finance for Key Workers

NHS, emergency services, armed forces and other key worker roles often bring stable income — here's how that plays with lenders.

The challenge

Key worker roles (NHS, police, fire, ambulance, armed forces and similar) often come with stable, verifiable income, which generally works in your favour with lenders — the main friction points tend to be irregular shift-pay structures or a need to prove overtime/allowances count toward income.

What tends to help

  • Payslips that clearly show base pay plus regular allowances/overtime help a lender assess your true income accurately.
  • Stable public-sector employment can sometimes unlock better representative rates through a broker's panel than applying to one lender directly.
  • If you're relocating for a role, make sure your address is updated with your bank and on the electoral roll before applying.
