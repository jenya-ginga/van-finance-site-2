'No Credit Check' Van Finance — What's Realistic

Be cautious of anyone offering finance with genuinely no credit check at all.

The situation

FCA-regulated lenders are required to assess affordability as part of responsible lending, which in practice almost always includes some form of credit or income check — so an offer of finance with genuinely no check at all is a red flag rather than a shortcut. What you're usually looking for isn't 'no check', but a lender that's more forgiving of a poor or thin credit file, or one that leads with affordability rather than a raw credit score.

What tends to help

  • Treat any lender explicitly advertising 'no credit checks' with caution — check they're FCA-authorised before providing any details or paying a fee.
  • Look instead for lenders that describe themselves as specialists in bad credit or non-standard credit, which is a genuine and regulated category.
  • Affordability-led lenders (using open banking, for example) can approve applicants a pure credit-score model would reject, without skipping checks entirely.
  • Never pay an upfront 'guarantee' fee to secure finance — this is a common scam pattern, regulated lenders don't require it.
