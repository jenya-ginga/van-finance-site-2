Van Finance with a Soft Credit Search

Checking your eligibility without leaving a mark on your credit file.

The situation

A soft search (or 'quotation search') lets a lender or broker estimate your likelihood of acceptance without leaving a visible footprint that other lenders can see, unlike a hard search which is recorded and can slightly affect your score, especially if you have several in a short period. It's a genuinely useful way to compare options before you commit to a full application.

What tends to help

  • Ask directly whether an eligibility check uses a soft or hard search before you submit any details online.
  • A soft-search 'likely to be accepted' result isn't a guarantee — the full application still involves a hard search and full underwriting.
  • Comparing several soft-search quotes is a low-risk way to shop around before choosing where to formally apply.
  • Only proceed to a full (hard-search) application with the lender that actually looks like the best fit.
