Van Finance Without a Guarantor

Don't have anyone who can act as guarantor? Here's what to look at instead.

The situation

Not everyone has a relative willing or able to act as guarantor, and that's fine — most UK van finance lenders don't require one. The applicants who struggle most without a guarantor are usually those with limited credit history or a lower income relative to the van's cost, so the practical fix is to strengthen the parts of the application you do control.

What tends to help

  • Put down as large a deposit as you can — it directly reduces the amount a lender has to take a risk on.
  • Choose a van priced comfortably within your budget rather than stretching to the maximum you might be offered.
  • Get on the electoral roll and register any utility bills in your name at a stable address before applying.
  • Use a broker with a wide panel — a single soft-search application can be matched against several no-guarantor-friendly lenders at once.
