About us

Built to make van finance easier to understand

My VanFinance exists because comparing van finance in the UK usually means ten browser tabs, five broker forms and no clear picture of the total cost. We put the tools, the lenders and the numbers in one place.

What we do

We're an independent information and comparison site covering van finance across the United Kingdom — Hire Purchase, PCP/balloon finance, and the lenders and brokers behind them. We don't lend money ourselves; we help you understand your options before you approach a lender directly.

The site brings together four things people usually have to hunt for separately: a rated hub of UK van finance lenders, free calculators that model real repayment scenarios, vehicle and licence-check tools to protect you before you buy, and plain-English guides on the topics that come up again and again.
