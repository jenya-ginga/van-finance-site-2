About Startline Motor Finance

Startline Motor Finance underwrites vehicle finance for customers who don't fit standard prime lending criteria, distributing exclusively through its network of accredited dealer partners. It places particular emphasis on affordability assessment over credit score alone.

Who Startline Motor Finance is best for

Self-employed tradespeople and small business owners buying through an accredited dealer, who want a lender that looks beyond a single credit score.

Pros
  • Affordability-first underwriting approach
  • Strong dealer network experienced with commercial vehicle buyers
  • Options for both personal and light business use vans
  • Established specialist reputation in the non-prime space
Cons
  • Only accessible through accredited dealers, not direct to public
  • Rates reflect non-prime risk pricing
  • Approval still requires passing an affordability assessment
