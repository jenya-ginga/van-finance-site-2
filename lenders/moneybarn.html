About Moneybarn

Moneybarn focuses exclusively on non-prime and near-prime motor finance, including vans and light commercial vehicles. Rather than a single flat rate, they price each application individually based on affordability and risk, which is why their representative APR range is wide compared with mainstream lenders.

Who Moneybarn is best for

Drivers who have been declined by mainstream lenders, have missed payments or defaults on file, or have limited credit history — for example newly self-employed tradespeople or recent UK arrivals.

Pros
  • Considers applicants most mainstream lenders decline
  • No minimum credit score threshold
  • Reports payments to credit reference agencies, helping rebuild credit
  • Flexible on vehicle age and mileage for commercial vehicles
Cons
  • Representative APR is higher than prime lenders
  • Missed payments can affect the vehicle-backed agreement
  • Not the cheapest option if you qualify for a mainstream rate
  • Application is a hard search, unlike some broker platforms
