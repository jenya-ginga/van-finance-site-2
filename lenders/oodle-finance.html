About Oodle Finance

Oodle uses open banking (with the applicant's consent) to look directly at income and spending patterns, which can help applicants with a limited or imperfect credit history demonstrate genuine affordability rather than being judged on a credit score alone.

Who Oodle Finance is best for

Buyers with a thin credit file, recent credit blips, or irregular self-employed income who can show healthy bank account activity even without a strong credit score.

Pros
  • Open banking affordability check can help thin-file applicants
  • No fixed minimum credit score
  • Clear, published representative APR range
  • Straightforward online application
Cons
  • Requires open banking consent, which not everyone is comfortable with
  • Representative APR sits above prime lenders
  • Vehicle age/mileage limits apply to qualifying vans
