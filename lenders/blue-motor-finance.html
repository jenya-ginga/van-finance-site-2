About Blue Motor Finance

Blue Motor Finance underwrites hire purchase agreements for used vehicles sold through its network of approved independent and franchise dealers, rather than lending directly to consumers browsing privately. Rates depend on vehicle age, deposit and credit profile.

Who Blue Motor Finance is best for

Buyers purchasing through an approved dealer who want a straightforward HP agreement without going through a broker first.

Pros
  • Fast dealer-side decisions, often same day
  • Straightforward HP structure, easy to understand
  • Works with a wide network of independent van dealers
  • Established track record in the UK used-vehicle finance market
Cons
  • Only available through approved dealers, not directly to consumers
  • Deposit requirement is higher than some competitors
  • Rate range still stretches into non-prime territory for weaker credit
