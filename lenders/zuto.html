About Zuto

Zuto doesn't lend money directly — instead it runs one application against a panel of finance providers and returns the offers you're likely to be accepted for, starting with a soft search that leaves no visible mark on your credit file. It covers cars, vans and motorbikes.

Who Zuto is best for

Buyers who want to compare multiple lenders in one go without repeated hard searches, and who aren't sure which type of lender (prime, near-prime or specialist) they'll qualify with.

Pros
  • Soft search shows likely offers before a hard credit check
  • One application reaches a wide panel of lenders
  • Consistently strong customer review volume and score
  • Dedicated support if your vehicle develops a fault post-purchase
Cons
  • You don't always know which specific lender you'll end up with
  • Final rate still depends on the underlying lender's pricing
  • Broker fee/commission is built into some offers, as with any broker
