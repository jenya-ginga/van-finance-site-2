Ford Van Finance

Compare finance options for the Ford Transit range — from the benchmark large Transit to the compact Transit Courier — and see how independent comparison stacks up against dealer finance.

About Ford's van range

Ford's UK van line-up covers four main models. The Transit is the full-size large van and the long-standing benchmark of the segment, used everywhere from parcel delivery to construction. The Transit Custom is the mid-size model and one of the most commonly seen vans on UK roads for trades and small businesses. The Transit Connect and Transit Courier sit at the smaller end, aimed at buyers who want car-like running costs with van-like practicality. The E-Transit is Ford's electric large van, aimed at fleets and businesses moving toward zero-emission driving.

Ford has built one of the UK's best-selling and most recognised van ranges over several decades, and the Transit name in particular is often treated as the reference point other vans are compared against. That long history also means Ford's dealer and service network is one of the largest and most widely spread in the UK, which matters for parts, servicing and warranty work wherever you're based.

Because so many Transits and Transit Customs are on the road, the used market is unusually deep. You'll find used Fords across a huge range of ages, mileages and specifications — from high-mileage ex-fleet vans at the budget end to nearly-new, low-mileage examples — so there's more room to shop around on price and condition than with rarer makes.

Financing a Ford van

If you're buying from a Ford dealer, you may be offered a manufacturer or dealer-arranged finance scheme as part of the sale. That's one legitimate route, but it's rarely the only one worth looking at — independent comparison sites like this one let you see how dealer finance stacks up against finance arranged through other lenders, without negotiating that separately yourself.

Whichever route you take, the things worth comparing stay the same: the representative APR, the deposit required, and the length of the term, since these three factors do most of the work in determining what you'll pay each month and overall. It's also worth checking whether the agreement is Hire Purchase or PCP-style, and what happens if you want to settle early. Our HP van finance calculator can help you see how changing the deposit or term affects the monthly repayment, and our affordability calculator can help you check what fits your budget before you apply.

Given how broad Ford's buyer base is — sole traders, small fleets, first-time van buyers — it's worth comparing lenders whose focus matches your situation. Moneybarn has built its reputation partly around vehicle finance for buyers whose credit history isn't perfect, which is relevant given how many Ford vans are bought by newer or smaller businesses. Zuto operates as a broker with access to a panel of lenders rather than a single finance product, so it can be a quick way to compare multiple options on a widely-financed van like a Transit or Transit Custom.

FAQ

Q: Can I get finance on a used Ford Transit?

A: Yes — used van finance is widely available for Transits, Transit Customs and other models, though the rates and terms on offer will depend on the lender, the vehicle's age, and your circumstances.

Q: Is dealer finance always the best option for a Ford van?

A: Not necessarily. Dealer or manufacturer-arranged finance is convenient, but it's worth comparing the representative APR, deposit and term against other lenders before deciding, since better terms may be available elsewhere.

Q: What's the difference between financing a Transit and a Transit Custom?

A: The finance process is similar regardless of model — what changes is the vehicle price and therefore the loan amount, deposit and monthly repayments, since the Transit Custom is generally lower value than the full-size Transit.
