Peugeot Van Finance

Peugeot's Partner, Expert and Boxer range covers small, mid-size and large van buyers, and comparing finance across several lenders — not just what's offered at the dealership — is the best way to make sure the deal matches your budget.

About Peugeot's van range

Peugeot's UK van range spans three sizes: the Partner is the small van, popular with tradespeople, delivery drivers and anyone who wants car-like running costs with a proper load area; the Expert is the mid-size option, a common pick for builders, plumbers and small businesses that need more racking and payload than a small van offers; and the Boxer is the large van, generally used for bulky loads, panel-van conversions and larger fleet operations.

Peugeot has a long history of fleet and commercial sales in the UK, and the brand is generally positioned as offering competitive pricing within its class — a general reputation built up over years of trade and fleet business, rather than a claim about any specific model or deal. Like its Vauxhall, Citroën and Fiat stablemates, the current Partner, Expert and Boxer are built on shared Stellantis platforms, so similar vans wearing different badges are often worth cross-shopping too.

With such a strong fleet footprint, the used market for Peugeot vans is typically well supplied, including plenty of ex-fleet and ex-lease stock across all three sizes. As with any used van, mileage, service history and how the vehicle has been used — courier work versus light trade use, for example — matter more to its condition and value than the badge on the front.

Financing a Peugeot van

Peugeot dealers and the brand's own finance arm are one route to funding a Partner, Expert or Boxer, and it's worth asking what's on offer if you're buying new or nearly new. But dealer finance is only one option, and comparing it against independent lenders can uncover a deal better suited to how you actually run your business or use the van day to day.

Whatever route you go down, compare like for like: the total cost of the agreement over its full term, the deposit needed, how long the term runs, any restrictions on mileage or modifications, and whether the product fits your intended use — sole trader, limited company, or personal. Our HP van finance calculator and affordability calculator are worth running the figures through before you sign anything, so you know what the repayments look like against your actual outgoings, not just the headline monthly figure.

It can also be worth getting a couple of quotes from lenders that specialise in vehicle finance rather than general credit. Zuto works as a broker that searches across a panel of lenders on your behalf, which can save time if you'd rather compare several offers through one application. Oodle Finance is a more recent, online-first lender in the vehicle finance space that tends to consider a fairly broad range of applicants — useful to have in the mix if your circumstances are a little outside what a mainstream high-street lender typically wants to see. As always, acceptance and terms depend on the lender's own assessment of your application.

FAQ

Q: Can I finance a used Peugeot van rather than a new one?

A: Yes — our panel lenders generally finance used vans too, though the deposit and term on offer can depend on the van's age and mileage.

Q: Does Peugeot's fleet reputation affect my finance options?

A: Not directly — a lender assesses the specific vehicle and your application rather than the brand's general fleet reputation, though a well-supplied used market can make it easier to find the exact spec you want at a price that suits your budget.

Q: Is it worth comparing lenders if my Peugeot dealer has already offered finance?

A: Generally yes — comparing a dealer's offer against independent lenders costs nothing and can confirm whether you're getting competitive terms or whether another lender suits your situation better.
